# 0001. The first maintainers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document type | Record (ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289), MADR |
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-11-01 |
| Deciders | AxonOps Limited, as copyright owner |
| References | [governance.md](../process/governance.md), [committers.md](../governance/committers.md) |

## Context and problem statement

The project needs its first maintainers: the people with rights across the project who approve every step by merging, approve releases, decide security disclosures, decide on commit rights and change the process. Under the governance rules, maintainers are appointed by AxonOps Limited as copyright owner.

## Considered options

1. Appoint the two people who set up the project and its process as the first maintainers.

## Decision outcome

Chosen option: "Appoint the two people who set up the project and its process as the first maintainers", because the project needs maintainers from its start and they have led its setup.

Johnny Miller (`millerjp`) and Hayato Shimizu (`hshimizu`) are maintainers of ChronitonDB from 2026-11-01, with the rights listed in [governance.md](../process/governance.md#two-levels).

### Consequences

- Good: every approval point in the process has people who can act on it.
- Bad: two maintainers are a small group. Further maintainers are appointed as the project grows.
